## Workshop workflow

During the session, we will move through the following steps:

```text
Protein / domain of interest
        ↓
Sequence retrieval
        ↓
Domain identification
        ↓
Multiple sequence alignment
        ↓
Alignment inspection
        ↓
Maximum-likelihood phylogeny
        ↓
Tree visualization
        ↓
Protein structure comparison
        ↓
Structural phylogeny
        ↓
Comparison of sequence and structural trees
```

Detailed practical instructions will be added to this repository.
